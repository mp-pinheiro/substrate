# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be direct, respectful, and constructive. Disagreement about code and design is welcome; personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, threats, and deliberate disruption are not.

Keep reviews focused on the work. Give people room to correct mistakes, and do not publish another person's private information.

## Scope

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces used to represent this project.

## Reporting

Do not open a public issue about a conduct incident. Use the repository's [private reporting form](https://github.com/mp-pinheiro/substrate/security/advisories/new) and begin the report title with `Conduct report`. Include relevant links or screenshots and say whether there is an immediate safety concern.

The maintainer will acknowledge the report, review the available evidence, and keep details private to the extent possible. Anyone involved in the report will be excluded from handling it.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, issue a warning, restrict participation, or ban someone from the project. The response will depend on the severity and pattern of the behavior.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
